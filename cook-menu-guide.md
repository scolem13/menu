# Menu Ideas for Sonali Didi

**Our needs, every dish:** vegetarian · little to no dairy (milk, curd, paneer, ghee) · very mild spice (toddler-friendly, minimal or no green/red chili) · less salt than usual

Everything below fits those rules — pick anything, mix and match, no need to ask each time once she knows the pattern.

---

## Dishes that DON'T need a mixer/grinder

These use only chopping, boiling, steaming, or a simple hand-mash — good for right now.

### Dal & rice mains
- **Plain moong dal** — yellow moong dal boiled soft with turmeric, light tempering (jeera, hing, curry leaves) in oil
- **Palak dal / palak khichdi** — moong dal + rice + chopped spinach cooked together (like the one already made)
- **Toor dal, simply tempered** — no chili, just jeera-hing tempering
- **Plain khichdi** — rice + dal + turmeric, soft for the toddler
- **Vegetable pulao** — rice with chopped carrot, peas, beans, mild whole-spice tempering (bay leaf, cinnamon, no chili)
- **Curd-free jeera rice** — plain rice with jeera tempering

### Sabzis (dry vegetable dishes)
- **Mixed vegetable sabzi** — cauliflower, carrot, beans, potato, chopped and sautéed with turmeric + light tempering
- **Cabbage sabzi** — shredded cabbage sautéed with ginger-garlic (finely chopped, not paste) and turmeric
- **Bhindi (okra) sabzi** — sliced and sautéed
- **Pumpkin sabzi** — chopped, sautéed or steamed then lightly mashed
- **Sweet potato / potato sabzi** — boiled and lightly sautéed with turmeric and jeera
- **Beans and carrot sabzi**

### Steamed / boiled
- **Steamed broccoli, cauliflower, carrot** — plain or with a light butter-free oil drizzle and pinch of salt
- **Boiled sweet potato** — as a toddler snack too

### Soups
- **Palak-dal soup** — spinach + dal boiled soft, mashed by hand with a ladle, with garlic, ginger, turmeric (like the one already made for sore throat)
- **Tomato-vegetable soup** — chopped tomato, carrot, boiled soft, mashed by hand (skip cream/butter finish)
- **Mixed vegetable soup**

### Snacks / breakfast (no mixer)
- **Poha** — flattened rice with peas, potato, light tempering, no chili
- **Upma** — semolina with vegetables, mild tempering
- **Store-bought idli batter, steamed** — as already done
- **Vegetable khichdi for toddler** — extra soft, extra mild

---

## Dishes that DO need a mixer/grinder (once it arrives)

### Batters & doughs
- **Homemade dosa/idli batter** — rice + urad dal, ground and fermented
- **Besan (chickpea flour) steamed dishes** — like the besan + Eno bread already tried, plus dhokla
- **Thalipeeth** — mixed-flour flatbread, mild version without chili

### Chutneys (can be made very mild — just skip the chili)
- **Coconut chutney** — coconut, ginger, roasted gram, light tempering
- **Coriander-mint chutney** — mild, no green chili
- **Tomato chutney** — mild, no chili

### Purees & smooth soups
- **Pureed vegetable soup** — carrot, pumpkin, or tomato blended smooth, good for the toddler
- **Smooth dal** — for a creamier texture without adding dairy

### Ground masala mains
- **Mild biryani** — the moringa powder + cashew + vegetable biryani already planned, using a ground ginger-garlic-herb paste instead of chili
- **Vegetable gravies (mild)** — onion-tomato base ground smooth, turmeric and mild spices only, no cream — coconut milk in small amounts if a little richness is wanted
- **Stuffed parathas** — vegetable stuffing ground/mashed fine (e.g., grated-and-ground carrot or cauliflower paratha)

## Handy phrases for her (Marathi / Hindi)

| English | Marathi | Hindi |
|---|---|---|
| Less spicy, please | कमी तिखट करा | कम तीखा बनाओ |
| Less salt, please | कमी मीठ घाला | कम नमक डालो |
| No milk / curd / ghee | दूध/दही/तूप नको | दूध/दही/घी मत डालो |
| For the baby, very mild | बाळासाठी, अगदी सौम्य | बच्चे के लिए, बहुत हल्का |
| Whatever you think is good | तुम्हाला जे बरे वाटेल ते | जो अच्छा लगे वो बनाओ |

Once the mixer arrives, just let her know — she can then also offer chutneys, dosa/idli batter from scratch, and smoother gravies within the same mild/low-salt/low-dairy limits.
